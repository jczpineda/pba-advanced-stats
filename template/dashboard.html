PBA 50th Season · Governors’ Cup Game 601 — —

Magnolia led for one possession all night

TNT won the perimeter and the ball, and that was the whole game — Magnolia actually won the glass and shot better inside the arc.

The Hotshots out-rebounded TNT 49–47, took more offensive boards (13–11), and were the better two-point team (52.1% to 50.9%). They still lost by 22, because they went 3-of-26 from three — 11.5% — and coughed up 15 turnovers to TNT’s 9. TNT turned that into a 26–15 edge in perimeter points and 13–8 in points off turnovers, with 17 assists against 8.

Magnolia’s only lead of the night was 29–28 with 5:31 left in the second. The game’s last tie came at 31–31; TNT answered with a 9–0 run, led by double figures from the opening minute of the third quarter onward, and peaked at +25.
